I'm Samuel Lowe (he/him), a mathematician, data scientist, and software engineer specializing in platform work and full-stack web development, based in the greater Boston area. I'm currently consulting as a data analyst at the [Center for Strategic Politics](https://stratpolitics.org/people/) and as the lead software engineer for [Oasis Management Inc](https://oasismanagementincorporated.com). At CSP I designed the weighted single transferable vote algorithm behind our [2025 New York City mayoral primary poll](https://stratpolitics.org/2025/06/after-heated-second-debate-and-superpac-attacks-mamdani-and-cuomo-race-to-statistical-tie/), which was [featured in the New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/polls/nyc-mayoral-election-polls-2025.html). At Oasis, I lead a team of four engineers building the Django platform and mobile app that run the business, now used by over 50 employees.

> **I'm looking for full-time or part-time work starting in January 2027.** You can see more on my [work experience](https://samlowe.dev/experience/) page or in my [resume](https://samlowe.dev/assets/pdf/resume.pdf), and reach me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/samclowe/) or at [sam@samlowe.dev](mailto:sam@samlowe.dev).

I graduated from Northeastern University with a B.S. in Computer Science and Mathematics in 2023 and an M.S. in Applied Math in 2025, mostly focusing on probability and number theory (specifically [elliptic curves](https://samlowe.dev/assets/pdf/elliptic_curves.pdf)). Some of my projects, like [nprcore.me](https://github.com/jordanbuchman/npr-core), have been recognized by [National Public Radio](https://twitter.com/npratc/status/1379189469014851589) and [All Things Considered](https://twitter.com/NPRinterns/status/1379191512773316612).
